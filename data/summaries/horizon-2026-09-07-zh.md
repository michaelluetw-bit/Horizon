## [Asahi Linux 支援 M3 晶片](https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

## [OpenAI 發佈 Astra 後多次改動評測數據，幻覺率先降後恢復](https://fortune.com/2026/09/04/openai-quietly-boosts-some-of-astras-evaluation-metrics-amid-rare-delay-in-publication-of-the-modeblog-post-announcement/) ⭐️ 8.0/10
