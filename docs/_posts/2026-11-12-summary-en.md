### [US Commerce Department Finalizes Proposed AI Chip Remote-Access Rule](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/commerce-department-finalizing-long-awaited-ai-remote-access-rule) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The US Commerce Department is reportedly finalizing a rule to prevent Chinese AI companies from remotely using advanced AI chips through overseas data centers. The proposal would also extend semiconductor export controls to seven countries, including Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand, to address remote access and chip smuggling. The rule could be announced as early as this month, but its final scope and effective date remain unclear. The White House previously asked Congress to require the department to create such a rule, while Nvidia lobbied against closing the remote-access loophole.
